## Why

The "We couldn't sign you in" card after a Microsoft sign-in that took too long offered "Try another Microsoft account". No account was refused, so that sends the person to change accounts for nothing. Siraj (19-Nov-2026): on that card the button reads "Sign in with Microsoft".

## What Changes

- A sign-in that took too long, or was started in another window, shows the first page's ways in exactly, with "Sign in with Microsoft" (or Google). Only a refused account is offered "Try another … account".
