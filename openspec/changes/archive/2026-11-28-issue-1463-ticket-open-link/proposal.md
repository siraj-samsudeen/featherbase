## Why

A MotherDuck review ticket names the account to link, but the reviewer has to find the Link MotherDuck accounts screen by hand (#1463).

## What Changes

- Any ticket's details may carry an open link: a label and a relative path on this site. Its page shows it as a button.
- The MotherDuck sync gives each review ticket a link to the Link screen at its account, and the umbrella one to the screen; tickets raised before get it on the next run.

### New Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `tickets-sub-tickets`: a ticket links to where its work is done.
- `motherduck-accounts`: review tickets open the Link screen.
