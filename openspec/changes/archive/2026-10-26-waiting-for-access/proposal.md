# Proposal

## Why

Staff from an allowed company domain get an account the first time they sign in with Google, but with no role, so they land on a page that says they have no access. Nobody is told, so they wait until they chase someone. System Managers need to see these people and hear about them as they arrive.

## What Changes

- A page for System Managers lists everyone who signed in but holds no role yet, newest first: their name, their email domain, how they signed in, and when they first and last signed in. Opening one goes to their person record to give them roles. A count beside the page's link in the menu shows how many are waiting.
- When a sign-in rule creates a new person, every enabled System Manager with an email address gets one short email: who, which domain, how they signed in, and a link to the page.
- A mail problem never stops the person signing in. Each person is announced once, and a day's emails are capped so a burst of arrivals cannot flood inboxes; past the cap the page is still the record.
