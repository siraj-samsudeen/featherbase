## Why

Some Tasker promises were written down before they were built. The main specs
now describe only what Tasker does today, so the unbuilt promises live here as
future work instead of being lost.

## What Changes

- Go through the Inbox one task at a time, seeing how many are left, placing or
  skipping each one and moving straight on to the next.
- When a task is set to Blocked, On hold or Cancelled from its details, offer
  the same optional explanation of why that task lists already offer.

Not included, and not yet promised: combining, splitting, archiving or deleting
projects, and placing many Inbox tasks at once.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `tasker-process-inbox-items`: one-at-a-time Inbox processing.
- `tasker-responsibility-and-progress`: the explanation prompt also appears in
  task details.

## Impact

The Tasker app only.
