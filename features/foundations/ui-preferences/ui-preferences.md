# UI preferences

The scene: a person is using a list or a form and wants it laid out their way.

## Acceptance criteria

The two sentences are from the design notes. Their tests were written by Claude while building the mock-up
screens for real, and are not yet walked through with Siraj.

1. A person chooses how a record opens, side panel or full page, and the choice is remembered.
2. A person chooses the row height, comfortable or compact, and the choice is remembered.

## Decided

Both layouts are kept; neither is the "real" one. With no sign-in in this build the choice is remembered in
the browser. It becomes a per-person setting when people sign in.
