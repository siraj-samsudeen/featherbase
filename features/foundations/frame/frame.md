# Frame

The scene: a person is on any page of Featherbase. The frame is what stays the same around every page: the
sidebar on the left and the page on the right. Its look comes from `features/foundations/pages/`.

## Acceptance criteria

Proposed by Claude while building the frame. Not yet agreed by Siraj.

1. Every page has the sidebar: Featherbase, Home, and each Table with its number of rows.
2. The sidebar marks where the person is: the Table whose list is open, otherwise Home.
3. On a wide screen the sidebar sits to the left of the page.
