# Uploading

The scene: a person has a CSV file and gives it to Featherbase.

## Acceptance criteria

Proposed by Claude while building the journey. Not yet agreed by Siraj.

1. A file given to Featherbase is kept: its proposal opens again from the sidebar, even after a restart.
2. Home lists each upload under "Recent Uploads" with its number of rows and the day it arrived.
3. Opening an upload Featherbase does not have shows "Page not found".
