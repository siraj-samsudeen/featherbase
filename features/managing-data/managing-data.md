# Managing data

A person looks through a Table's rows, and adds, changes and deletes them. One list and one form serve every
Table.

Steps: `browsing`, `changing`. They share one screen, the list with its form, in
`features/foundations/pages/screens.html`.

Later, not in this build: search, sort and filter; a guard against two people editing one row; soft delete as
a switch; active and inactive rows.
