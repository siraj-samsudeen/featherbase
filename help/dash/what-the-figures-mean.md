# What the figures mean

## Sales

Sales are before tax and net of returns, for this month so far.

## Target

The target is the sales target before tax for the same days.

## Gap

The gap is sales less target. A negative gap means behind target: −₹10,000
means ₹10,000 short. "Furthest behind" lists only what is behind target, the
largest shortfall first.

## Achievement

Achievement is sales as a percentage of target: 100 × sales ÷ target. With no
target there is no achievement, and it is shown as "—", never as zero.

## Change and growth against last month

The change is this month's sales less last month's, over the same days of the
month. Growth is that change as a percentage of last month's sales. A
subcategory that sold nothing last month has no growth, shown as "—".

## How recent the figures are

Each answer says the day the figures run to and when they were refreshed. The
figures are refreshed once each morning, so today's sales are not in them yet.

## A row with "—" or "No section"

"—" means there is no figure: for example a subcategory with a target but no
recorded sales. "No section" means the subcategory sold but no section of the
store owns it; its sales still count in the store's totals.
