# Whose figures you see

## Your stores and sections

You see the figures of the stores and sections you are responsible for today,
from the store org chart. A Store Manager sees the whole store. A Department
Manager sees the sections of their department. A Team Leader sees their
sections, and section staff see the sections they work in.

Every answer FeatherBot gives covers the same stores and sections as your report,
and no others. Under each answer, "Covers" says which.

## Why can't I see a store or section?

You see a store or section only while the org chart gives it to you. If one is
missing, ask your Store Manager to check your job in the store org chart.

## When you see no store at all

If the page says you have no store yet, your job is not in the store org chart,
or your store's figures are being prepared. Ask your Store Manager.

## Naming a store or section in a question

You can name your own stores, sections and subcategories in a question, in
capitals or not. Naming one you do not see finds nothing: the answer says the
name matched nothing of yours.
