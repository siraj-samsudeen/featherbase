# Sending a request for review

In **Action** mode you can ask for something to be reviewed by a person. The
chat never changes anything itself, and never decides anything.

## Asking for a target review

Say what you want, for example "Please review the Kids Wear target at Lakeside;
the stock arrived two weeks late." A target review needs the store, the
subcategory and the reason. If you leave one out, the chat asks for it. If you
can see only one store, it is filled in for you. You can ask only about stores
and subcategories you can see.

## Nothing is sent until you say yes

The chat shows the request it would send. Press **Send Request**, or say yes, to
send it; press **Cancel**, or say no, and nothing is sent. To change something,
just say what to change. One conversation sends at most three requests: start a
new conversation for more.

## Where a request stands

Each request gets a number, such as TR-12. Press **Requests** at the top of the
panel, or ask "what have I sent?", to see yours: waiting for review, approved or
rejected, with the reviewer's note.

## Reviewing requests

If you review requests, **Requests** also shows what others have sent and is
waiting, oldest first. Approve or reject each, with a note for the sender if you
like. You never review your own requests, and a request is decided once.
Approving a target review does not change the target by itself: whoever sets
targets acts on it, as before.
