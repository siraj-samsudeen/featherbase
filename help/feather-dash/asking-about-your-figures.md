# Asking about your figures

FeatherBot, the chat panel beside the report, answers questions about your
sales in your own words, such as "Which subcategories are furthest behind
target?" or "How do my sales compare with last month?". Pick a mode under the
box, then ask. You see only the modes you have been given.

## Agent and Reports

In **Agent** mode the answer appears in the chat: a few words, then the table.
In **Reports** mode the answer is drawn as a report on the page, and the chat
says so; press **Show it again** to bring an earlier one back.

## What you can ask

You can ask about sales, targets, the gap between them and achievement, by
store, section, department or subcategory, for this month so far, and you can
compare this month with the three months before it. Each month is counted over
the same days of the month as this month so far, so a month in progress is
compared fairly.

The questions shown in an empty conversation are ones it is known to answer
well. Press one to ask it.

## Follow-up questions and earlier conversations

After an answer you can ask a follow-up, such as "and by section?" or "only the
top 3". Press **Sessions** to see your earlier conversations, open one and carry
on, or remove it from your list. Press **+** to start a new conversation. Your
conversations are yours alone, and are there on any computer or phone.

## When it asks you a question back

If a question could mean two different things, for example sales in rupees or
in pieces, it asks you which you meant instead of guessing. Answer in the box.

## When it cannot answer

If the figures cannot answer a question, for example a question about profit,
it says so in plain words. Try asking it another way, or about sales, target,
gap or achievement. If the chat says this month's allowance is used up, it opens
again on the first of next month.

## How an answer was worked out

Under each Agent answer, open **How these figures were worked out** to see what
each figure means and the SQL that worked the answer out.
