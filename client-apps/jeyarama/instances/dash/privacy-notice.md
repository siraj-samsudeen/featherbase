# Feather Dash: Privacy notice

*Effective 23-Oct-2026*

**Feather Dash** is the internal operations and reporting system of **Jeyarama Retail Business Group**. Only employees and authorised staff use it. It is not a public or customer-facing service.

## What we hold about you

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and employee code | to identify you and show you your own work |
| Your StyleHR user ID | to let you sign in with your StyleHR details |
| Your work email address and Google account ID (if you sign in with Google) | to identify you; Google shares only your name, email address and account ID with us |
| Your store, sections and role (e.g. Team Leader, Store Manager) | to show you the reports and records that belong to you |
| A record of each sign-in: time and sign-in method | security and troubleshooting |

## What we do not hold

- **Your StyleHR password.** It is passed to StyleHR once, over an encrypted connection, to check it, and is never stored or logged by Feather Dash.
- **Your Google password.** Google checks it; we never see it.
- Anything about you from outside the company.

## Where the information comes from

From the company's HR records (StyleHR and HR's staff files), and from what you or an authorised manager enter in Feather Dash.

## Who can see it

- You can see your own details.
- Your managers see what their role covers: a Store Manager for their store, a DM for their sections.
- A small number of system administrators, for support.
- The information is copied into the company's data warehouse for reporting.

It is **never sold or shared** with anyone outside the company, other than the service providers that host the system for us:

- **Railway**, for the application and database, hosted in **Singapore**.
- **MotherDuck**, for the data warehouse, hosted in the **United States**.

## When access ends

Your access follows the company's HR records. When you leave the company, your Feather Dash account is disabled, which ends every way of signing in, including Google. Sign-in with your StyleHR details also stops as soon as HR marks you as having left in StyleHR, and Google sign-in stops when your company Google account is closed.
