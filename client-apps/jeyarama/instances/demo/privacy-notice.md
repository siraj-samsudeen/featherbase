# Privacy notice

This is a public demonstration of Featherbase. Everything in it, Banyan Retail, its stores, people,
figures, tasks and tickets, is made up. No real person is behind any account.

- Anyone may sign in with the published demo accounts and see what those accounts see.
- Don't type real personal information, passwords or business data into it: other visitors can read it.
- No email leaves this instance.
- The demo is reset from time to time, and everything visitors added is then removed.
