Who may do what, and where

Every question about access comes down to four answers. Each answer has one home, and every screen can say which home it came from.

1What does HR call them?Assistant Manager – OperationsDesignation, from StyleHR. Never edited here.
2What does the business call them?Store ManagerBusiness role, from the title map, a merchandise assignment, or set by hand.
3What may that role do?Use Dash, view reports, give reports to their teamThe bundles the business role includes.
4Where?ATK, the whole storeThe place on that same assignment. A role never travels without its place.

Three levels, never deeper

Postgres lets a role contain roles without limit, and then nobody can say why a person can do something. Here there are exactly three levels, and each is a different kind of thing.

level 1
Business role
Store Manager · Floor Manager · Team Leader · Staff · Cluster Manager · Buyer · Category Head · Head Office · Master Data Steward
What people are called at work. These are given to people, and nothing else is. One business role never contains another.
level 2
App and level
Dash · Reader · Dash · Team sharing · Dash · Report admin · Tasker · User · Tasker · Manager · Master data · Editor
This is the "give these apps to these people" step. Each app offers a few levels, and having any level means you can open the app, so no "Tasker Member" role is needed. A business role picks its apps and levels. Odoo works this way.
level 3
Permission
Reports: View · Reports: Give to my team · Sections: Edit · Section changes: Approve
Ships with the app, because only the app's code can carry it out. Never names a place: "all stores" is a place, not a permission.

Above all three: the admins

  • Instance admin (today "System Manager"): installs apps, manages people and sign-in.
  • App admin, one per app: edits that app's bundles and settings, nothing else. Does not exist yet.
  • Both are given by hand, never from an HR title.

Where the starting set comes from

  • Shipped: each app's permissions and suggested bundles, plus a retail starter set of business roles.
  • Jeyarama's own: the starter set is copied in at setup, then edited here. Each role shows a tag: from starter changed our own.
  • A store or department gets no settings of its own. A store manager may be allowed to give existing roles to people in their store.

Explain a person

Pick someone. This is the screen that answers "why can she see this?". Each assignment keeps its role and its place together. The people are made up; their titles are the kind StyleHR holds.

What a business role can do

One row per thing. View, Add, Edit and Delete are fixed columns; anything that isn't one of those is a plain verb on the same row. Pick a role.

Bundles and ticks are suggestions for discussion, not decisions.

When the title is wrong: two different buttons

Correct

Replaces the role the title gave. Use it when HR's title misleads: "treat this person as Store Manager at ATK".

Also give

Adds a second role, with a reason and an end date. Use it for a real second hat: "also Master Data Steward until March".

Nothing ever takes away. Access only adds up, so one screen can always list every reason.
