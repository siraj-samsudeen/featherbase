Who may do what, and where

A person's access is a short sentence: a business role, which lets them do something to a thing, as far as their reach goes. The reach is the only part that changes between a buyer and a director.

1What does HR call them?Assistant Manager – OperationsDesignation, from StyleHR. Never edited here.
2What does the business call them?Store ManagerBusiness role: from the title map, a merchandise assignment, or by hand.
3What may they do?Edit sectionsBasic, plus what the business role adds.
4How far does it reach?Area: ATKOwn, Team, Area or All. The area comes from the assignment.

Four words for reach

Every permission is a thing, a verb and one of these four words. No permission is named after a place. "Edit sections" is a single permission; the store manager's assignment decides which sections.

Own

What I created or am assigned to. My tasks, my tickets.

Team

Mine plus the people who report to me, down the reporting line.

Area

Everything in my assignment's area: my store, my category, my section. Read from where the assignment sits on the axes below.

All

The whole company. Only a few roles, and every one of them is named.

Explain a person

This screen answers "why can she do this?". Each assignment keeps its role and its area together. The people are made up; their titles are the kind StyleHR holds.

What a role can do

Everyone with an HR record gets Basic: the everyday apps, working on their own things. A business role only adds to Basic, usually by widening the reach on a few rows. Pick a role. Rows it changes are listed underneath.

Reaches and rows are suggestions for discussion, not decisions.

One word per thing

Site, store and plant are not three names for one thing. They are three related things, and each keeps one name on screen. The Glossary lists the other names people use, and search finds a thing by any of them.

Above every role: the admins

  • Instance admin (today "System Manager"): installs apps, manages people and sign-in.
  • App admin, one per app: changes that app's rows in this grid, and nothing else. Does not exist yet.
  • Both are given by hand, never from an HR title.

Changing a person by hand

  • Correct: replace the role the title gave ("treat as Store Manager at ATK").
  • Also give: add a role, or one app at Basic for a consultant, with a reason and an end date.
  • Nothing ever takes away, so one screen can list every reason.

Decisions

  1. Agreed 17-Nov: three levels, business role → app access → permission. A person can be given an app directly as an exception.
  2. Reach as four words (Own, Team, Area, All), with the area read from the assignment's place on the axes.
  3. One Basic bundle for everyone with an HR record.
  4. One word per thing: Site, Store and Plant as above.
  5. "Correct" and "Also give" as the only hand actions.
  6. A starter set copied in at setup, not live layers.
  7. Merge Data Quality Manager into Master Data Steward.
