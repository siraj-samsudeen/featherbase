Report an issue from a Dash report

Issue #1240. A reader who sees something wrong on a report says so in a few taps; the ticket already carries the report, the view, their stores and sections, the Dive's filters, who they are and when. Three ways to draw it, each at 1440×900.

Chosen: A, a "Report an issue" button in the report header that opens a side panel inside Dash. The report stays in view beside the form, so the reader can point at the number they mean while they write. The button sits in the header like every page action in the UI standard, and never covers the Dive. The confirmation and the reader's open reports on this report fit in the same panel. B covers part of the Dive and adds a floating control nothing else in Featherbase uses. C takes the reader away from the number and into Tickets' general form, and the view travels in the address.
